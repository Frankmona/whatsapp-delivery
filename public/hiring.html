Frequently Asked Questions (Rider Vacancy)

Do I need to own my own bike?

We have commercial motorcycles and fleet support available for qualified riders. If you already have a bike in good condition, you also receive additional maintenance allowances.

How is payment disbursed?

You receive a guaranteed monthly salary plus commissions earned per delivery. Commissions can be paid weekly or bi-weekly directly to your bank account.

What areas will I cover?

Our station hub is based in Kubwa. Deliveries range from intra-Kubwa runs to central districts (Wuse 2, Maitama, Jabi, Utako, Garki, Gwarinpa, Central Area).

When do interviews start?

Immediately. Once you message us on WhatsApp with your documents and credentials, shortlisted candidates are invited for a physical interview and route test in Kubwa.
