How FM XPRESS Bulk Dispatch Works

No complicated apps. Your customers order from you as usual, and we handle the delivery run.

1

Pack & Tag Your Orders

Prepare your daily packages (food trays, pastries, groceries) and attach simple name tags with customer name, phone number, and delivery location.

2

Send Us Your Manifest

Send your delivery list directly to our dispatch WhatsApp or portal in one message. We automatically sequence the route and dispatch a dedicated rider with insulated delivery bags.

3

Live Tracking & Delivery

Each of your buyers instantly gets a WhatsApp message with their personalized live tracking link. As the rider drops off each meal, you get real-time confirmation.
